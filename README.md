# Finance Researcher

> Clear, practical research on personal loans, credit, debt, mortgages, budgeting, and fintech.

Finance Researcher is an open collection of educational notes and long-form guides designed to make everyday financial decisions easier to understand.

The project focuses on the questions that matter before someone borrows money:

- What does the loan really cost?
- How does APR differ from the headline interest rate?
- Can the monthly payment fit a real household budget?
- How do fees and repayment terms change the total cost?
- What should a borrower compare before accepting an offer?

## Featured guide

A practical, step-by-step framework for comparing APR, fees, monthly payments, loan terms, total repayment, lender terms, and affordability.
