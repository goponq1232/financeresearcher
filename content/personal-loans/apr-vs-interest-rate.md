# APR vs Interest Rate

The interest rate shows the basic cost of borrowing money.

APR can provide a broader measure because certain finance charges may be reflected in it.

## Example

| Feature | Loan A | Loan B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Interest rate | 9% | 10% |
| Origination fee | 5% | 0% |
| Term | 36 months | 36 months |

Looking only at the rate makes Loan A appear cheaper, but fees can change the comparison.

A useful next step is to review the total scheduled repayment and the amount that actually reaches your bank account.
