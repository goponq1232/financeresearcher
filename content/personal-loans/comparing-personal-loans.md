# How to Compare Personal Loan Offers

A useful loan comparison looks beyond the advertised rate.

Compare:

1. **APR**
2. **Origination fees**
3. **Monthly payment**
4. **Repayment term**
5. **Total repayment**
6. **Net proceeds**
7. **Late-payment and prepayment rules**

A lower monthly payment can simply mean a longer repayment period.

For a deeper explanation of borrowing costs, see [EasyLoanWorld](https://easyloanworld.com/).
