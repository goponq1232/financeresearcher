# Privacy Questions Before Using a Lending App

Digital lending can make applications faster, but convenience should not replace privacy checks.

Before providing sensitive information, ask:

- What data does the app collect?
- Why does it need each permission?
- How is financial information stored?
- Is information shared with third parties?
- Can you delete or export your data?
- Does the company provide clear contact and privacy information?

Financial decisions increasingly involve both money and data.
