# The True Monthly Cost of a Mortgage

Principal and interest are only part of the cost of owning a home.

Depending on the property and loan, the monthly housing cost can also include:

- Property taxes
- Homeowners insurance
- Mortgage insurance
- HOA fees
- Maintenance reserves

When comparing affordability, evaluate the complete monthly housing cost rather than only the mortgage payment shown in a calculator.
