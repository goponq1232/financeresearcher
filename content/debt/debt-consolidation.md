# Debt Consolidation: What to Compare

Debt consolidation can simplify several payments into one, but simplification does not automatically mean savings.

Before consolidating, compare:

- Existing credit-card APRs
- New loan APR
- Origination fees
- Monthly payment
- Repayment term
- Total repayment
- What happens to the paid-off cards

The biggest behavioral risk is rebuilding card balances after moving the old balances into a personal loan.
