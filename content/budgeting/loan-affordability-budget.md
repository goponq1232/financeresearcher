# Build a Loan Affordability Budget

Lender approval and personal affordability are different things.

Start with take-home income and subtract:

- Housing
- Food
- Transportation
- Utilities
- Insurance
- Existing debt
- Savings
- Irregular expenses

Then stress-test the proposed payment.

## Example

Take-home income: **$4,800**

Normal expenses and savings: **$4,250**

Remaining: **$550**

A $500 loan payment technically fits, but leaves almost no room for an unexpected expense.

A better question is:

> Can I make this payment during a difficult month without borrowing again?
